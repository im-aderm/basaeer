Admissions

What are the age requirements for enrollment? expand_more
For Kindergarten, children must be 4 years old by September 1st. For Grade 1, the requirement is 6 years of age. We follow a strict age-appropriate placement policy to ensure social and emotional readiness.
Are there scholarships available for talented students? expand_more
Yes, Basaeer Model School offers Merit-Based Scholarships for students excelling in academics and the Tahfeez program. Financial aid is also available through our Zakat-funded scholarship pool for eligible families.

Academics

What curriculum does Basaeer follow? expand_more
We integrate the rigorous British International Curriculum (Cambridge) with our proprietary Islamic Integrated Science program, ensuring students are competitive globally while remaining spiritually grounded.

Tahfeez Academy

Is the Tahfeez program integrated into the daily schedule? expand_more
Yes, we offer two paths: Full-time Tahfeez (with core academics) and Integrated Tahfeez (morning sessions). Both options are led by certified Qaris with a focus on Tajweed and memorization retention.

General

Does the school provide transportation? expand_more
Basaeer provides air-conditioned transportation services covering a 20km radius around all campuses. All buses are equipped with real-time GPS tracking and trained supervisors.
